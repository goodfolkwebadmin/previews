How it works

Four simple steps. You keep running your business. We handle your Google presence.

  1. Get your free audit

    Send us your business name and profile. In about 10 minutes we review it the way a customer would.

  2. See your gaps

    We walk you through what is missing, what is out of date, and what could be costing you calls. In English or Spanish.

  3. We fix them

    Hours, photos, categories, contact details, and reviews. We clean it all up so your profile is complete and accurate.

  4. We keep you growing

    Every month we keep your profile fresh and active, so it keeps working long after the first cleanup.

Questions owners ask

What happens during the free audit?

We look at your Google Business Profile the way a customer would and check things like your hours, photos, contact details, categories, and reviews. Then we show you what is missing and what we would fix first.

Do I have to sign up for a plan after the audit?

No. The audit is free, and you are welcome to use what you learn however you like.

Can we work together in Spanish?

Yes. Choose English or Español on the audit form and we will talk with you in that language.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $300 to $400 per month for Basic, $400 to $600 for Standard, and $600 to $800 for Premium. Your price depends on your business, and we confirm it with you before any work begins.

What is missed-call text-back?

When you cannot answer the phone, the caller automatically gets a text from your business, so a new customer does not slip away just because you were busy.
