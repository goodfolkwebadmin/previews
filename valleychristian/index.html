Your first Sunday

Come as you are. We'll save you a seat.

Never been to church, or just new to town? Here's what to expect when you walk in.

About two hours, with plenty of hellos

Service starts at 10:30am and usually wraps up around 12:30pm. Come a few minutes early to find a seat and say hello.

Hymns, new songs, and the Bible

Worship that blends contemporary songs and traditional hymns, and a message from the Bible you can use on Monday.

Room for the whole family

Kids are part of who we are. Wear whatever you're comfortable in, straight from the orchard or the ballgame is fine.

Questions first? Call (541) 938-7161, Tuesday and Thursday 9am to noon.

Let us know you're coming

Since 1928

We love the culture of family. We want it to grow.

People thrive when they're connected to God, cared for in a family, and growing in faith. So we work to make sure everyone we meet has met Jesus (because He's a real person!), has friends who encourage them, and has what they need to keep building their faith.

Valley Christian Center is part of the Assemblies of God fellowship. Read what we believe.
