Welcome home

We love the culture of family. We want it to grow.

We believe people thrive when they are connected to God, nurtured in family, and growing in faith. So we work hard to make sure everyone we come across has met Jesus (because He's a real person!), has developed relationships with encouraging people, and has the resources and direction to keep building their faith.

"God sets the lonely in families..."Psalm 68:6

Every Sunday morning we gather to:

WorshipJesus
EngageHis Word
Ministerto the World

Valley Christian Center is part of the Assemblies of God fellowship and has served the Milton-Freewater community for more than 75 years. Read what we believe.

Plan your visit

Your first Sunday, made easy.

Never been to church, or just new to town? Come as you are. Here's what to expect when you join us.

When

Our Celebration Service starts Sundays at 10:30am and usually wraps up around 12:30pm. Arrive a few minutes early to grab a seat and say hello.

What it's like

Worship that blends contemporary songs and traditional hymns, a message from the Bible you can apply to everyday life, and people who are glad you came.

Kids

Families are at the heart of who we are, and we have ministry for children too. Let us know you're coming and we'll help your kids feel at home.

Where

800 N Main St in Milton-Freewater, right on Main Street. Look for a friendly face at the door and we'll point you in the right direction.

What to wear

Whatever you're comfortable in. Come straight from the field, the orchard or the ballgame. You'll fit right in.

Questions?

Call the church office at (541) 938-7161 during office hours, Tuesday and Thursday 9am to noon, or send us a note.

Let us know you're coming.

Tell us a little about yourself and we'll save you a seat, answer your questions and make sure someone is there to welcome you.
