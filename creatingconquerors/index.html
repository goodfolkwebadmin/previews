Who we are

Prevention, recovery, and hope for the next generation.

Creating Conquerors gives young people in our community real alternatives, real support, and real stories from people who have walked the road to recovery.

Our mission

Preventing substance abuse and suicide among youth and young adults in Umatilla County and beyond.

Our vision

Empowering individuals in recovery to share their story to help others on their life journey.

What we do

Four ways we show up for our community.

Recovery stories

Real people sharing their journey through addiction and recovery, so young people know there is a way through.

  • Prevention and recovery videos
  • Podcast
Watch on YouTube →

Treatment services

Outpatient substance use disorder treatment, with care that meets people where they are.

  • Screening and ASAM assessment
  • Individual and group sessions
  • Case management

Training and support

Equipping parents, professionals, and community members to step in and help.

  • ASIST suicide intervention training
  • Triple P Positive Parenting
  • Clinical supervision (CADC, CRM)
  • Business development and coaching for organizations

Healthy activities

Positive outlets that keep kids active, coached, and connected to people who care.

  • Dream Team Softball Training Facility
  • Pitching and hitting lessons
