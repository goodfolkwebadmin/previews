Greg and Patti have been at this since the waterbed days.

Greg started with a specialty sleep store in The Dalles and was one of the first retailers there to sell waterbeds, right alongside innerspring beds. When he and Patti opened in Hermiston, the store became known for waterbeds and bedroom furniture.

Waterbeds came and went. The store grew into living rooms and dining rooms, took on a bigger name to match, and sleep is still what they know best.
