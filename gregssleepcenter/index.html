From a waterbed shop in The Dalles to Sofas 'n More

Greg was one of the first retailers in The Dalles to sell waterbeds, right alongside innerspring beds. The store grew, then grew again, and somewhere along the way it stopped being only about the bedroom.

Sleep is still what we know best. We've just added a lot more places to sit.

  1. 1976Greg's Sleep ShoppeA specialty sleep store opens in The Dalles.
