A little piece of San Juan, Michoacán, in Hermiston

Mercado San Juan is a family-owned business started in 2015 by Antonio and Olga Rauda. As first-generation business owners who moved here from Hillsboro, we saw a chance to bring a little piece of home to Hermiston.

The market is named after San Juan, Michoacán, the pueblo where Antonio was born and raised, and where we still go often to be with family.

For us it's not just about food. It's about building friendships and serving our community. Thank you for being part of our story.

Antonio & Olga RaudaFounders
